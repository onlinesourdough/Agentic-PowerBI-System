# Power BI project brief

- **Business question:** What must the report answer?
- **Decision:** What will someone do differently with the answer?
- **Audience and owner:** Who decides, and who owns the result?
- **KPIs:** Name, definition, source, owner, format, target, interpretation, and
  known limitation.
- **Data:** Canonical systems, tables, grain, relationships, freshness, quality,
  privacy, and access boundaries.
- **Refresh and runtime:** Desktop, gateway, workspace, schedule, capacity, and
  operational owner.
- **Report behavior:** Pages, filters, drill paths, loading/no-data/error/stale
  states, accessibility, and mobile requirements.
- **Design handoff:** Optional path to an approved Design-template bundle and the
  decisions it settles.
- **Proof:** The real question, validation, refresh, access, and decision journey
  that must pass.
- **Non-goals:** What the first complete result deliberately excludes.
