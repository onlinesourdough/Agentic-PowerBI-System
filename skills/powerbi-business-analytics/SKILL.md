# Power BI Business Analytics

Use this skill when the task is about business analysis, KPI design, hypotheses, dashboard storytelling, or translating business context into Power BI work.

## Workflow

1. Clarify the business question:
   - decision maker
   - decision/action needed
   - grain of analysis
   - relevant dimensions
   - success metric
2. Translate to a model requirement:
   - facts, dimensions, relationships
   - required measures
   - filters/slicers
   - data quality assumptions
3. Translate to report design:
   - page purpose
   - 3-30-300 structure: KPIs, explanation, detail
   - chart choices
   - annotations/conclusions
4. Write business interpretation, not just visuals:
   - what happened?
   - why does it matter?
   - what should the user do?
   - what are the caveats?

## Retail / supermarket KPI patterns

- Revenue, profit, margin %, basket size, transactions, average order value
- Category mix, store comparison, time trends, campaign effects
- Top/bottom stores, outliers, weekday/weekend effects
- Contribution analysis: share of revenue/profit by category/store

## Output standard

Always separate:

- **Business question**
- **Metric definition**
- **DAX/model impact**
- **Report visual/page impact**
- **Interpretation**
- **Limitations**
